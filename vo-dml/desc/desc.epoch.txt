The \texttt{epoch} package (fig \ref{fig:epoch}) groups the data types
used by \texttt{mango:EpochPosition} epoch.
The package is based on types duplicated from \cite{2022ivoa.spec.1004R}
and extended with 2 Mango types (\texttt{Years} and \texttt{Seconds))
All the concrete types are derived from \texttt{coords:InstanceTime}.
