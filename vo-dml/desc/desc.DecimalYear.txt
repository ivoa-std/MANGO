Fractional number of years AD represented by the date. 2000.0, or equivalently 1999.99recurring, 
is midnight at the start of the first of January 2000. Because of leap years,
the size of a unit depends on what year it is in 
(see \url{https://www.star.bris.ac.uk/~mbt/topcat/sun253/sun253.html#Times}).
