A continuous measure based on a tropical year of about 365.2422 days
($BesselianEpoch = 1900.0 + \frac{jd - 2415020.31352}{365.242198781}$).
For approximate purposes this resembles the fractional number of years AD represented by the date.
Can be represented by prefixing a 'B'. 
(see \url{https://www.star.bris.ac.uk/~mbt/topcat/sun253/sun253.html#Times}).
