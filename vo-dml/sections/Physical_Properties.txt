This section brings together all the classes needed to operate a hook on 
objects imported from the \texttt{Measurements} data model \citep{2022ivoa.specQ1004R}. 
The Measurements model contains 6 classes that model simple physical quantities 
 (Position, Time, Velocity, Polarisation, ProperMotion, GenericMeasure) in a very versatile way,
supporting many coordinate systems and a wide variety of errors.

It is recommended to use them for quantities that do not require the use of native MANGO classes.
